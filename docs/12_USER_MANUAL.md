# 1. Introduction

## Purpose

This User Manual provides step-by-step instructions for using NexaMart AI. It is intended to help customers, vendors, and administrators understand the platform's features and complete common tasks efficiently.

---

# 2. System Overview

NexaMart AI is a responsive multi-vendor e-commerce platform that enables customers to purchase physical products from multiple vendors while allowing vendors to manage their stores and administrators to oversee the platform.

The system supports:

- Customer shopping
- Vendor store management
- Product management
- Shopping cart
- Secure checkout
- M-Pesa payments
- Order tracking
- Notifications
- Reviews
- Administrative management

# 4. Accessing NexaMart AI

1. Open a supported web browser.
2. Navigate to the NexaMart AI website.
3. Select Login or Register.
4. Enter the required information.
5. Sign in to access your dashboard.

# 6. Customer Guide

## Register an Account

1. Click Register.
2. Enter your full name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Create a secure password.
6. Verify your email address.
7. Sign in.

---

## Login

1. Open the Login page.
2. Enter your email.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click Login.

---

## Browse Products

Customers can:

- Browse categories.
- Use the search bar.
- Filter products.
- Sort results.
- View product details.

---

## Add to Cart

1. Open a product.
2. Select the desired quantity.
3. Click Add to Cart.

The shopping cart updates automatically.

---

## Checkout

1. Open the shopping cart.
2. Review your items.
3. Click Proceed to Checkout.
4. Enter shipping information.
5. Choose a payment method.
6. Confirm the order.
7. Complete payment.

---

## View Orders

Customers can:

- View order history.
- Track delivery status.
- Download invoices.
- View payment status.

---

## Wishlist

Customers can:

- Save products.
- Remove products.
- Move products directly to the shopping cart.

---

## Reviews

After receiving an order, customers can:

- Rate products.
- Write reviews.
- Edit submitted reviews where permitted.

# 11. Security Guidelines

Users are encouraged to:

- Create strong passwords.
- Keep login credentials confidential.
- Log out from shared devices.
- Enable two-factor authentication if available.
- Avoid sharing verification codes.

Administrators should regularly review user permissions and monitor audit logs.

# 12. Troubleshooting

## Cannot Login

Possible causes:

- Incorrect email
- Incorrect password
- Unverified email
- Network connection issues

---

## Payment Failed

Possible causes:

- Insufficient funds
- Incorrect payment details
- Network interruption

Retry the payment or contact your payment provider if the issue persists.

---

## Product Not Available

The selected product may:

- Be out of stock.
- Have been removed by the vendor.
- Be temporarily unavailable.

---

## Slow Performance

Possible causes:

- Slow internet connection
- Browser cache
- Temporary server maintenance

Refreshing the page or trying again later may resolve the issue.

# 13. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my password?

Yes. Passwords can be updated from the Profile Settings page.

---

### Can I cancel an order?

Orders may be cancelled before they enter the shipping process, subject to the vendor's cancellation policy.

---

### Can vendors edit products after publishing?

Yes. Vendors can update product information at any time, provided the changes comply with platform guidelines.

---

### Can I track my order?

Yes. Order tracking information is available in the My Orders section.

---

### What payment methods are supported?

The platform supports M-Pesa and any additional payment methods configured by the administrator.

---

# 14. Support & Maintenance

For technical assistance, users should contact the platform administrator or support team through the designated support channels.

Regular maintenance may temporarily affect system availability. Planned maintenance periods should be communicated to users in advance whenever possible.
