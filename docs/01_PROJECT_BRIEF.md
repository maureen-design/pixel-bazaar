# 1. Introduction

NexaMart AI is a modern AI-assisted multi-vendor e-commerce platform designed to connect customers with vendors through a secure, responsive, and user-friendly online marketplace. The platform enables multiple vendors to register, manage their stores, and sell products while providing customers with an intuitive shopping experience.

Unlike traditional e-commerce systems, NexaMart AI focuses on usability, scalability, and intelligent shopping features while maintaining a clean and professional user interface.

---

# 2. Project Overview

The project aims to develop a web-based marketplace where customers can browse products, place orders, manage wishlists, and track purchases. Vendors can manage products, monitor sales, and process orders through a dedicated dashboard, while administrators oversee the entire platform by managing users, vendors, products, reports, and system settings.

The system will be fully responsive, ensuring a consistent experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Many small and medium-sized businesses struggle to establish an online presence due to the high cost and complexity of developing and maintaining individual e-commerce websites.

Customers also face challenges when shopping across multiple independent stores because product discovery, order management, and payment experiences vary significantly between platforms.

A centralized marketplace that allows multiple vendors to sell their products through one secure platform provides a practical solution for both businesses and customers.
